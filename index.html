Projects

High Ground Clearance Suspension Arm

Designed arched lower front suspension arms for the UW–Madison Baja SAE team, improving ground clearance over the previous model. Performed FEA in SolidWorks to evaluate performance under multiple loading conditions and refined the design to meet the targeted factor of safety. Collaborated with teammates to fabricate prototype arms and conducted offseason durability testing for endurance racing validation.

Formula One Logo LED Sign

Created a custom LED sign by modeling in Autodesk Inventor for CNC machining and exporting to VCarve. Machined the acrylic base, soldered red LEDs into three connected loops, and added connectors for standard outlet compatibility. Integrated electrical and mechanical components to produce a durable, functional display.

Cutting / Charcuterie Boards

Designed cutting board and charcuterie board templates in Fusion 360. Processed raw materials using planer and jointer, and executed multiple glue-ups for complex patterns. Completed finishing through progressive sanding and grain-popping techniques, applying a food-safe mineral oil finish for durability and moisture resistance.

Bolt Action Pen

Modeled a bolt-action pen in SolidWorks and generated engineering drawings with defined tolerances using a stack-up analysis. Produced a fully functional 3D printed prototype emphasizing precision, fit, and aesthetics before finalizing design. Machined stainless steel components using bandsaw, 3-jaw and collet chuck lathes, and a vertical mill.

Laser Cut Layer Art

Designed layered silhouette artwork in Autodesk Inventor for laser cutting. Fabricated and hand-finished each layer through sanding and painting, then assembled and framed the pieces for a polished presentation. Combined CAD, manufacturing, and finishing techniques to create functional art pieces.

Pool Cue Stand

Designed a cedar pool cue holder in Fusion 360 with integrated storage for balls, chalk, and racks. Fabricated using mortise-and-tenon and rabbet joints, plus precision drilling on a drill press. Finished with rounded edges via router and applied a protective polyurethane coat for durability and aesthetics.
