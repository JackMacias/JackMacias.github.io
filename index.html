Projects

High Ground Clearance Suspension Arm

Designed an arched lower front control arm to boost ground clearance without sacrificing strength. Built the SolidWorks model, ran FEA to tune wall thickness and fillets, and designed a jig for repeatable weldment. Machined threaded inserts to mount a load cell and capture track data, then correlated measurements to the model to validate safety factors and stiffness.

Formula One Logo LED Sign

Reverse‑engineered the F1 logo in Autodesk Inventor and CNC‑machined the body with integrated channels for LED strips. Soldered and wired three addressable loops with clean strain relief and a hidden connector for standard wall power. Added a diffuser layer and tidy back‑side cable management for a uniform glow and serviceable assembly.

Cutting / Charcuterie Boards

Custom boards in walnut, maple, and cherry focused on clean joinery, contrasting grain, and durable finishes. Flattened and glued up panels, routed profiles, and sanded through grits before applying a food‑safe oil/wax finish. The set shows edge‑ and end‑grain patterns, inlay accents, and consistent thickness control.

Bolt Action Pen

Modeled a bolt-action pen in SolidWorks and generated engineering drawings with defined tolerances using a stack-up analysis. Produced a fully functional 3D printed prototype emphasizing precision, fit, and aesthetics before finalizing design. Machined stainless steel components using bandsaw, 3-jaw and collet chuck lathes, and a vertical mill.

Laser Cut Layer Art

Designed layered silhouette artwork in Autodesk Inventor for laser cutting. Fabricated and hand-finished each layer through sanding and painting, then assembled and framed the pieces for a polished presentation. Combined CAD, manufacturing, and finishing techniques to create functional art pieces.

Pool Cue Stand

Designed a cedar pool cue holder in Fusion 360 with integrated storage for balls, chalk, and racks. Fabricated using mortise-and-tenon and rabbet joints, plus precision drilling on a drill press. Finished with rounded edges via router and applied a protective polyurethane coat for durability and aesthetics.
